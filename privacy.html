Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

illumina Software ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications including Hong Kong Mahjong. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our games and services.

1. Information We Collect

Our games are designed to provide entertainment without requiring invasive personal details. We may collect non-personally identifiable information automatically through trusted third-party services:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID).
  • Game performance metrics, crash logs, and diagnostics.
  • In-game preferences, themes, and coin balances stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We use industry-standard SDKs to support our multiplayer infrastructure, crash analytics, and monetization:

  • Google AdMob: Serves personalized and non-personalized advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Collects anonymous crash telemetry to help us diagnose bugs and maintain app stability.

3. Multiplayer Gameplay

When participating in realtime multiplayer games, your chosen in-game display name, avatar, and discard actions are transmitted securely over WebSocket to enable synchronized gameplay with other players in the room.

4. Children's Privacy

Our games comply with applicable children's privacy regulations. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
