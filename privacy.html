1. Information We Collect

Our design philosophy is to minimize data collection. We do not require you to register with personal real-world identities (such as your full legal name, home address, credit card number, or phone number) to enjoy the core single-player or multiplayer modes.

A. Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party SDKs

When you access or play the game, third-party services and advertising networks may automatically receive certain technical and device data:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID/GAID), IP address, unique device identifier (pseudonymous).
  • Device and System Specifications: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language settings, screen resolution, and available RAM.
  • Diagnostic & Telemetry Data: Crash reports, application performance metrics, render engine diagnostics, and network latency status.

B. Game State and Local Preferences

  • Local Storage (PlayerPrefs): In-game sound effect volume, background music toggle, preferred table cloth color, tile face styles, chosen language (Indonesian or English), and virtual score records are saved locally on your device storage.
  • Multiplayer Matchmaking & In-Game Actions: When you connect to online multiplayer rooms, transient gameplay packets (such as your chosen display name, avatar index, and tile discard/meld actions) are transmitted securely over WebSocket to enable real-time game synchronization with other participants at the table.

4. Advertising ID & User Opt-Out Choices

You have full control over the advertising identifiers utilized by third-party advertising networks on your Android device:

  • Reset or Delete Android Advertising ID:
    On your Android device, navigate to Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). From there, you can tap "Reset advertising ID" or choose "Delete advertising ID" to restrict personalized ad tracking across all apps.
  • Non-Personalized Ads:
    When personalized ads are turned off or unavailable, Google AdMob serves contextual or non-personalized advertisements that do not target your behavioral profile.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain gameplay and telemetry data only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy:

  • Local Data Removal: Since your settings, game history, and preferences are stored locally on your mobile device, you can permanently erase all local game data at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > Mahjong 3D > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Cache, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Multiplayer Transient Data: Multiplayer room states and discard events are held in volatile server memory only for the duration of the match and are discarded after the room closes.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If you have questions regarding any server logs or wish to request data deletion associated with your advertising identifier, please email our support team at illumina.dev.soft@gmail.com.
